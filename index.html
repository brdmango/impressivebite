Four things that make a lab worth keeping

None of this is exotic. It's just what a lab relationship is supposed to feel like and usually doesn't.

A date, not a range

You get a delivery date when the case is accepted, and we tell you the same day if anything threatens it. Scheduling the seat appointment shouldn't be a gamble.

One number per unit

The quote is the invoice. No shade-match surcharge, no custom-abutment upcharge discovered at the end, no rush fee for a case that was always due Friday.

A technician who knows you

The same technician takes your cases, learns how you prep and what your patients expect, and picks up the phone. Not a queue, not a ticket number.

Remakes without the argument

If the fit is on us, we remake it and cover the shipping both ways. You shouldn't have to build a legal case to get a crown that seats.

How it works

Four steps, and three of them are ours

The practice does the clinical work. We do everything between the scan and the seat.

Scan the prep — or take the impression

Intraoral scan is ideal and gets the case moving immediately. Still working in PVS? Send the physical impression with our prepaid label and we'll digitize it on arrival.

Upload the case and the Rx

Drag the file in, pick the restoration, shade and material, add a note for the technician. It takes about two minutes, and nothing is faxed.

Approve the design

You see the proposed restoration before anything is milled — margins, contacts, occlusion and emergence. Approve it or send it back with a note.

It arrives before the appointment

Tracked shipping, with the delivery date you were given when the case was accepted.

Questions

What practices ask before sending the first case

Which scanners do you accept files from?

All the common ones. We work from standard open formats — STL, PLY and DCM — which every current intraoral scanner can export, some directly and some after enabling open export in settings. If you're not sure how to get files out of yours, tell us the model on the contact form and we'll walk your team through it.

Do we have to switch everything over at once?

No, and we'd rather you didn't. Most practices start by sending us a few single units while their current lab keeps the rest, then move more across once they've seen how the cases come back. There's no minimum and no contract, so there's nothing to unwind if you decide against it.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per unit and depends on the restoration and material, so we quote it rather than publish a number that would be wrong for half the practices reading it. Tell us your case mix on the contact form and we'll send a full price list — one price per unit, with what's included spelled out.

Who actually designs and makes the restorations?

Licensed dental technicians, working from your scan and your Rx. Design software does the geometry; a person makes the judgment calls about margins, contacts and emergence, and a person inspects the unit before it ships.

Can you work with our implant system?

Tell us the system and platform when you submit, and we'll confirm the components before starting. If it's something we don't stock, we'll say so before the case is in motion rather than after.

How do cases get to you and back?

Digital cases upload — nothing physical moves. For impressions, models or anything that has to travel, we send prepaid labels to the office so shipping isn't something you pay for or think about.
