Why practices switch

Built around the four ways an impression fails

Every property below exists because of a specific remake we've watched happen. Nothing here is decorative chemistry.

Reads through moisture

Intrinsically hydrophilic from the unset state forward, so sulcular fluid and a stubborn bleeder don't turn into voids at the margin.

Tears clean, or not at all

High tear strength in the wash layer means thin interproximal fins survive removal instead of staying behind in the sulcus.

Sets when it says it will

Working and set times hold across the temperature swings of a real operatory — no early snap on a July afternoon in Phoenix.

Pours the same on day 14

Dimensional stability lets a case sit over a long weekend or ship cross-country and still seat when the crown comes back.

Questions

The things practices ask first

Will this work with the guns and tips I already have?

Yes. Our 50 mL cartridges use the standard 1:1 dispensing format, so they fit the dispensing guns already in most U.S. operatories. Our mixing tips fit the cartridges, and standard tips fit ours. Nothing about switching requires new hardware.

Is there a minimum order or a contract?

No minimum and no contract. Order a single pack if that's what you need. Free ground shipping kicks in at $250, and established practices can request net-30 terms at checkout or through a rep.

How do I know which viscosity combination to order?

It depends on technique. One-step dual-arch usually pairs Heavy Body with Light Body. Two-step putty-wash pairs Putty with Extra-Light Wash. Single-material and implant cases often run Monophase alone. The technique guide maps each combination, or tell us what you do and we'll match it.

Can our lab pour it the same way?

Yes — it's a standard addition-cure silicone. Labs pour it with the same stone and the same timing they use for any VPS. Wait 30 minutes after removal before the first pour, and the impression stays pourable for up to two weeks.

Do you sell to DSOs and group practices?

We do. Multi-location groups get volume pricing, consolidated invoicing, and per-location shipping. Mention your location count on the contact form and we'll put together a quote.
