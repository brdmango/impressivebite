Before you ask

Common questions about getting started

How quickly can we send a case?

Same day, usually. Setting up an account takes a short conversation to confirm your scanner, your preferences and billing — after that the portal is live and you can upload immediately.

Do we have to leave our current lab?

No, and most practices don't at first. Run both. Send us a few units, keep the rest where they are, and compare on your own cases. There's no minimum, so there's no cost to taking your time.

What if we don't have a scanner?

Send impressions. We'll mail prepaid labels, digitize them when they arrive, and run the case through the same workflow. It adds about a day. If you're considering a scanner, tell us which ones — we'll give you an honest read on how each one's files behave in a lab.

How does billing work?

Monthly invoicing on net terms for established practices, or card at the case level if you'd rather. Either way the quote is the invoice — no line items appear that weren't agreed in advance.

Can you match what our current lab does?

Usually, and it's a good conversation to have up front. Send us a recent case and tell us what you like about how it came back — contact tightness, occlusal scheme, how the ceramics are characterized. We'll put it on your account as a standing preference.

Do you work outside the United States?

Not currently. We take cases from licensed practices in all 50 states and the District of Columbia.
